In this project, I set out to investigate the loss of 18-to-35-year-olds moving from Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk County) to one of the five boroughs (NYC), and quantify the potential financial impact of that on 
local spending.

This was done to support Ballpark Figure Booking's report titled "Saving Long Island: The Impact of Arts Programming on Economics & Quality of Life"
